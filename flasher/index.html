Efflorescence

Install the firmware

For the first flash of a new board, over USB. A lamp that already runs Efflorescence updates itself from its Settings page.

You need
  • A Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C3.
  • A TTP223 touch sensor: its GND to D1 (GPIO3), I/O to D2 (GPIO4), VCC to D3 (GPIO5). The board powers it from those pins.
  • A MOSFET module with an optocoupler input that switches the LED's minus: its GND to D4 (GPIO6), PWM to D5 (GPIO7).
  • Chrome or Edge on a computer. Firefox and Safari cannot talk to serial ports.

If the lamp has a battery wired to the board's 5 V pin, disconnect it before plugging in USB.

Install
  1. Connect the board's USB port to the computer.
  2. Press the button, pick the serial port in the dialog, confirm.
This browser cannot access serial ports. Use Chrome or Edge, or flash with esptool below. Serial access needs HTTPS. Open this page over https://. Reinstalling keeps the settings and the schedule unless you choose "erase".
Then
  1. The LED lights up. On your phone, join the open Wi-Fi network Efflorescence-XXXX. The setup page opens by itself; if not, open http://192.168.4.1/.
  2. Pick your home Wi-Fi, enter the password, save. The lamp restarts and is reachable at http://efflorescence-xxxx.local/ or at the address your router gave it.
  3. The setup network goes away after five minutes to save the battery. Hold the sensor to bring it back.
If the port does not show up
  • Use a data cable, not a charge-only one.
  • Close other programs that may hold the port, such as a serial monitor.
  • A lamp that is switched off is asleep and leaves the USB bus: tap the sensor to turn it on, or hold the board's BOOT button while pressing RESET.
Without a browser

The same image, efflorescence-full.bin, flashed with esptool:

esptool --chip esp32c3 -p PORT write-flash 0x0 efflorescence-full.bin

A lamp on the network takes efflorescence-app.bin instead, from the Settings page or with curl --data-binary @efflorescence-app.bin http://LAMP/api/v1/ota.
